LiveSlate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LiveSlate is made by Digital AIM. Contact: btbrepresenta@gmail.com

What LiveSlate collects

If you turn on alerts, LiveSlate stores your device's push notification token, the teams you follow, the games you choose to follow, and your alert settings on our alert server, so we can send you the alerts you asked for. That's all. LiveSlate has no accounts and doesn't collect your name, email, location, contacts or browsing history.

Your teams and settings are also saved on your phone. Scores and schedules are loaded from public sports data feeds.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. LiveSlate does not show ads.

Turning alerts off

Turn off notifications for LiveSlate in your phone's Settings, or uninstall the app. Our server deletes a device's data automatically once its push token stops working.

Watch buttons

Tapping Watch opens another company's app or website, such as ESPN or Peacock. Their privacy policies apply there.

Children

LiveSlate is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
